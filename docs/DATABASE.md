## Money rule
All money is stored as `bigint` in the currency's smallest unit (cents, kobo, ...; the number of decimals is per currency and lives in `currencies.minor_unit`), beside a `currency` text column. In DB0 it references `currencies(code)`; on a shard it is checked to the ISO 4217 shape `^[A-Z]{3}$` (a shard cannot reference DB0) and the server accepts only currencies enabled in `currencies` that have a row in `plan_prices`. A payment is in the currency of the paying owner's country (`users.country` -> `countries.currency`). No `numeric`, `real`, `double precision` or `money` column exists (the self-test fails if one is added). Conversion to the provider's unit happens only in `toProviderAmount` / `fromProviderAmount`. ## Open items
- Reference data: `currencies`, `countries` and `plan_prices` ship EMPTY. The build seeds `currencies` and `countries` from cited sources (ISO 4217, CLDR) and the provider's supported-currency page; the OWNER supplies `plan_prices` for every currency to be sold (PRD Q12). A currency without a price row is not sold; its countries are not offered at sign-up (no fallback currency).
- App role: `030-app-role.sql` creates the non-owner role (`DB_APP_USER`, password `DB_APP_PASSWORD`); `selftest-app.sql` proves it cannot change the ledger or the schema. Owner URLs (`DB0_ADMIN_URL`, `DB1_ADMIN_URL`) are for setup and `prisma db pull` only.
- `subscriptions.provider_authorization_ref`: depends on OPEN Q6; if kept, protect it (encrypt or tokenised reference only).
- `payment_intents` (global) vs `ledger_entries` (shard): they can disagree after a crash; a repair job must reconcile them. DECISION to confirm with the owner.
- Retention: purge jobs cover `purge_log`, expired tokens, `push_tickets`, `notification_log`; ledger rows are never purged.
