# Tax notes: VAT, invoices and receipts (draft, NOT verified)

Status: research notes from secondary sources read on 18 Oct 2026. This is not legal or tax advice. A qualified Nigerian tax adviser must confirm everything here before Rowta takes real money. Until then Rowta uses the payment provider's TEST mode only, so no real sale or VAT arises.

## What Rowta does and does not do
- Rowta issues **payment receipts** ("This is a payment receipt, not a tax invoice."). The word "invoice" is not used in the app, emails or code.
- Rowta does **not** calculate or charge VAT, hold a tax identification number (TIN), number invoices in a legal series, or send anything to a tax authority (no e-invoicing).
- Receipts show plan, seats, amount in the currency's smallest unit formatted for display, date, a masked payment reference and the renewal date. No card details.

## What the sources say (each is secondary; [VERIFY] against the enacted text and the tax authority)
| Topic | Finding | Source and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| VAT rate | Standard rate stays at 7.5% | BDO article dated 31 Mar 2026; Baker Tilly Nigeria |
| Law and date | Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Tax Administration Act took effect on 1 Jan 2026; the old VAT Act stopped applying after 31 Dec 2025 | BDO (same article) |
| Small companies | Exempt from filing monthly VAT returns and cannot charge VAT on invoices; the VAT-specific threshold was not stated in the pages I read | BDO. A search result suggested the threshold is disputed (50 million versus 100 million naira); I did not read it |
| Invoice contents | One vendor blog lists: supplier TIN, name and address, business registration number, sequential invoice number, date of supply, purchaser name, gross amount, VAT charged and rate; cites Section 153 of the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 | invoicedataextraction.com, 11 Mar 2026. A vendor blog, not the statute |
| E-invoicing | The sources disagree. BDO: a system planned for taxpayers with turnover of 5 billion naira and above, others later. Baker Tilly: all VAT-registered businesses are required to adopt sanctioned e-invoicing | BDO and Baker Tilly. Confirm with the tax authority |
| Digital services | A secondary source says non-resident suppliers above USD 25,000 turnover from Nigeria must register and charge VAT, with place-of-consumption indicators; the source had a date inconsistency | vatcalc.com, Sept 2025 |

## Questions for the tax adviser
1. Is the business (or the school-project operator) VAT-registered, and does the small-company exemption apply?
2. When real money is taken, must receipts become VAT invoices, with which fields, and is e-invoicing required at our turnover?
3. Are digital subscriptions sold to customers outside Nigeria treated differently?
4. Does the payment provider issue its own receipt or invoice, and does that already satisfy the rules?

## Owner actions before taking real money
Get the answers above, then decide whether to add a TIN, a legal invoice series and VAT lines. That is a change to the PRD and needs a decision; do not add it silently.
